# Changelog

## v0.1.2

- Waypoints to the entrances of the Raven Hill crypt, the Lushwater Oasis cave and the path up to
  Darkcloud Pinnacle.
- Secrets of the Dreamers points to where the book actually is, in the Cavern of Mists.
- The boat to Kalimdor is no longer skipped while you are still in the Eastern Kingdoms.
- A final step marks the guide as done.

## v0.1.1

- Every book is now routed for both factions: Ataeric (the Sepulcher), The Lessons of Ta'zo (Orgrimmar) and
  Archmage Antonidas (Ironforge) can be collected by Alliance and Horde alike. Steps in enemy territory carry a warning.
- Flight steps wait until you arrive, so boarding the wrong flight no longer advances the guide.
- The route only uses flight points a character can learn.

## v0.1.0

- First release: a RestedXP guide per faction that collects every library book on WoW Forever and hands them in.
- Books already turned in are skipped, along with the flights and boats that only lead to them.
